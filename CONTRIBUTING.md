# How to contribute to Zappy ? ⚡

If you wish to contribute to Zappy, follow these instructions to the end (do not skip anything or something bad could happen).

## 1) JIRA
Even though we hold Github Projects in high esteem, we find that [JIRA](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira) is much more powerful when it comes to anything that isn't related to issues (we call them *Tickets* over here).

### Create an Atlassian account
Follow a tutorial online, it takes about 15 mins to setup and then you are done for life.

### Send us an email describing why you want to contibute.
This requests can take up to 7 business week to be validated (you have been warned)

## 2) Claim Tickets
Open JIRA, ask us if there are free tickets, or simply assign yourself directly on which task you want to work.

## 3) Pull Requests
We enforce a strong policy regarding PRs, to be validated, it has to go through our CI/CI pipeline, in order to perform simple tests, if your program does not compile, your PRs will not be able to be merged until you satisfy the pipeline.

*"Praise the almighty pipeline"*
